Welcome to the research group of **Prof. Guijin Zou** in the Mechano-X Institute and Applied Mechanics Laboratory, Department of Engineering Mechanics at **Tsinghua University**. Our group works at the interface of solid mechanics, materials science, and biology, with a focus on **mechanomaterials** — the proactive design of material properties and functionalities through mechanics.

Research themes
======
- **Mechanomaterials & nanomechanics** — designing material properties and functions from the bottom up.
- **Solid mechanics & nanotwinned materials** — plasticity, strengthening, and toughening mechanisms across scales.
- **Cell & membrane mechanics** — interactions of nanomaterials with lipid membranes, vesicle morphogenesis, and membrane-targeting nanomedicine.
- **Peptide materials** — phase separation, coacervation, and self-assembly of bio-inspired adhesive and structural peptides.

We combine theoretical modeling, multiscale simulation, and close collaboration with experimentalists to understand and engineer how materials deform, assemble, and function.
